## Why

Open Design의 다크 에디토리얼 랜딩을 Afterglow의 실제 Svelte 루트 홈페이지에 이식하면서 인증·mockup·런타임 브랜딩 계약을 유지한다.

## What Changes

Port the approved dark editorial research-cloud landing into Afterglow’s real Svelte root presentation while preserving the destination app’s authentication, mockup, and runtime-branding contracts.

The scope is limited to:

- design-system-first editorial surface tokens;
- the Svelte root presentation;
- the twelve approved editorial SVG plates;
- the GitHub-star browser request and its CSP allowance;
- focused browser and automated tests; and
- the existing Unreleased landing entry.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- Dark editorial landing presentation at the Svelte root route.
- Approved twelve-plate editorial asset surface.
- GitHub-star status presentation permitted by the narrowly scoped CSP allowance.

### Modified Capabilities

- Root-route presentation, while retaining existing authentication, mockup destination, and runtime-branding behavior.
- Design-system tokens and focused verification coverage for the editorial surface.

## Impact

The public root page gains the approved editorial landing experience and its referenced runtime assets. Existing authentication, mockup routing, and runtime branding remain the integration contracts; the focused tests and existing Unreleased changelog entry are updated to record the change.
